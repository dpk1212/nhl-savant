Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026 · Applies to nhlsavant.com

NHL Savant (“we”, “us”) provides sports analytics and account features at nhlsavant.com. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can reach us.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, name, and email address from Google Authentication.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, product interactions, device/browser basics, and approximate location derived from IP (via analytics providers).
  • Subscription / billing metadata — plan status and related billing identifiers from our payment processor (we do not store full card numbers).
  • Support messages — whatever you voluntarily send to our support email.

2. How we use information

  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • Deliver the product (boards, picks views, account settings)
  • Process subscriptions and prevent abuse
  • Understand product performance and fix issues
  • Respond to support requests and important service notices

3. Sharing

We share data with service providers that help us operate the product (for example: Google Firebase / Authentication / Analytics, hosting, and payment processing). We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the service and our users.

4. Data retention

We keep account and subscription records for as long as your account is active and as needed for legal, security, and accounting purposes. Analytics data is retained according to our analytics provider settings.

5. Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time from the product.
  • You can request account deletion or a copy of your account data by emailing support.
  • You can revoke Google access to NHL Savant in your Google Account permissions.

6. Children’s privacy

NHL Savant is intended for adults who are legally allowed to use sports-betting-related informational products in their jurisdiction (generally 18+/21+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
