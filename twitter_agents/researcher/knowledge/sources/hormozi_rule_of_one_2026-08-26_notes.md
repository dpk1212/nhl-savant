## Core (verbatim operational intent)

- Anti-pattern: stack every great thing so “one of them will work” → **toss salad copy**.
- Rule: one big idea — **easy to understand, easy to believe, interesting or unique**.
- Subpoints / stories / facts / arguments exist only to **reinforce** that singular concept — not alternate pitches.
- USP = Rule of One (one selling proposition, not 19).
- Hard part = selecting the one idea (forces tact). Once selected, copy is simple/transparent.
- Best converting ads tend to be simplest / most direct / clearest.
- Hormozi keeps it written at the top of his computer; reports crisper, more targeted copy since.
