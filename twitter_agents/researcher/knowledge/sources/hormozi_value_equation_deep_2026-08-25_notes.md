## Business type

**Supply-constraint** vs **demand-constraint**.  
Hormozi prefers **demand-constraint** businesses (he’s good at creating demand/value/conversion).  
Core book framework this maps to: **value equation** — create value so you can charge as much as possible *and* convert as many as possible.

---

## Value equation (full fraction)

```
                Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
Value  ≈  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                         Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice
```

### 1) Dream outcome — category filter

Separates whether someone is **even interested** in your category.  
Status-linked examples: men ↔ make more money; women ↔ look better (generalizations he used).  
**B2B > B2C price** often because closer to **ROI** (dream tied to money back).

**Same dream, wild price spread:**  
$5 PDF vs $50,000 liposuction — both “don’t like how my stomach looks.”  
Difference = the other three variables.

### 2) Perceived likelihood of achievement (= inverse of risk)

Same procedure: fresh med-school surgeon vs 10,000 five-star surgeries → you pay the premium for certainty.  
**Guarantees** further decrease risk on top of proof.

### 3) Time delay

When they buy → when they get the dream.  
PT: arm-wrestle for $2k package because six-pack is 12–18 months out.  
Lipo: sleep → wake up thinner → far higher value from speed alone.

### 4) Effort & sacrifice (two sides of one coin)

- **Effort** — things you must **start** doing that you hate (wake early, be sore, new grocery/meal prep…).  
- **Sacrifice** — things you must **stop** doing that you love (Taco Tuesday, sleeping in…).

**Method to build a grand slam offer:**  
Itemize **every** step immediately before/after purchase that:
- increases risk  
- makes it take longer  
- forces hated work  
- forces giving up loved things  

Then **create a solution for each category**. Granular > vague “we help you lose weight.”  
That’s how you get stepwise company jumps (2→10 in a year) changing **what the offer says**, not the core ops.

## Offer enhancers (on top of the equation)

| Enhancer | Mechanism |
|----------|-----------|
| **Scarcity** | Limit units |
| **Urgency** | Limit time |
| **Guarantees** | Reverse risk (4 types below) |
| **Bonuses** | Drive short-term buying decisions; stack solves each friction |

### Four guarantee types

1. **Unconditional** — money back if they ask  
2. **Conditional** — guaranteed if they do X/Y/Z  
3. **Performance / implied** — “if you don’t make money, I don’t” (aligned skin)  
4. **Anti-guarantee** — lean into *no* guarantee: “if you need a guarantee, this isn’t for you” (filters)

### Bonus stack (sales, not discounts)

- Build bonuses that each solve a friction from the itemized list.  
- **Ask on the core offer first.**  
- If no → diagnose the **constraint** → plug the matching bonus (may need 2–3 to clear).  
- **Add value, don’t discount** — sales team stops racing to lower price.  
- **Surprise & delight:** fast buyers who don’t need bonuses still get remaining bonuses post-purchase (“since you bought…”). Hard closes get bonuses on the call; easy closes get them after and love you.  
→ Higher close rate without giving away margin on price.

## Sharp Flow application (itemize → solve)

**Dream:** Win more — sized when proven money + conviction are real; fade/flat when losers loud; without becoming sharp.

**Likelihood / risk:** Public grade (can’t hide) · wallet ROI/records · size vs usual · for/against · 5u+ print book · trial · cancel · money-back (map which guarantee type we actually offer).

**Time delay:** Phone at lock · before tipoff · before market moves — not 12 months of “becoming sharp.”

**Effort to remove (hate-work):** Stacking 3–4 subs · building datasets · film/Discord grind · decoding tips · guessing units · hunting steam/limits yourself.

**Sacrifice to avoid forcing:** They still get to bet (the part they like) — we don’t make them quit betting; we change *when* they press.

**Bonus/stack ideas (if sales needs them):** lock-alert setup · “how to read size vs usual” · unit-tier guide · public grade explainer — plug to constraint, don’t discount weekly price first.

**Paywall must show:** dream × certainty ÷ (delay × hate-work) — not a feature grid.
