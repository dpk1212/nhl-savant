## 2 · Laws / formulas

### Qual
Two opposing forces. Magic is in the middle. Hormozi personally sits on the **volume** side — not empty volume.

### The Algorithm (Elon / Tesla-president book — Hormozi-claimed; not independently verified)
Order is sacred:

1. **Question the requirements** — Why must these things happen, in this order, this long? Three questions: **What does that mean? How do you know that? So what?**
2. **Delete** so much you have to **add some back**. Most process steps are reversible doors.
3. **Simplify / optimize** — most for the least. Even if a step is +5% quality, if it is 40% of cost, kill it. **1.5 at 95% > 1 at 100%** when the team has other work.
4. **Accelerate** — pull timelines up. Meeting close = **Who · What · When**. Then: how much actual time-on-task? Why not sooner? Increase communication cycles (end of day ≫ end of week — Hormozi: “7xes the speed”).
5. **Automate LAST.** AI first is the trap. Litmus: *Though I am using AI — more views? more leads? more money?* Token cost ↑ and money not ↑ = blowing money on shit that doesn’t matter.

### Education → commerce (two requirements)
1. **Decrease comprehension cost** — talk in words other people understand.
2. **Increase conviction** the payoff is real — so the gamble of following is worth it.

### Truth
> The truth sells the most. The truth converts. It has to be the **whole** truth.  
> Say where you look bad. Then they believe where you look good.

Domain-scoped authority: Hormozi will not teach happiness. Scale / sell = him.
