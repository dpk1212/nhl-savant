## Core: four elements of value

**Dream outcome** = baseline. What does the person actually want to have happen?  
Every product/service has one. That’s the starting value before discounts.

Then three **discount / multiplier** factors applied to that dream:

| Factor | Question | Direction |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Speed** | How fast does it happen? | Faster = more value |
| **Effort** | How much work on the customer? | Less effort = more value |
| **Likelihood** | How likely is it to occur? (risk) | Higher certainty / risk-free = more value |

**Operator mantra:** How do I make it **fast**? **Easy**? **Risk-free**?  
Those either multiply what you can charge — or dettract from it.

---

## Likelihood / risk-free (proof + terms)

Ways to decrease risk (which reverse into price):

- **Proof volume** — “I’ve done this for 10,000 people” → less risky
- **Performance / covenants** — shift risk to seller
- **Satisfaction guarantees**
- **Conditional guarantees** — based on things *they* do

**Pricing play Hormozi described:**  
Offer at $5K with guarantee → they hesitate → drop to $3K *without* guarantee → they often resell themselves back to **$5K with the guarantee**.  
People buy the risk-reversal component; removing it makes them feel the value of it.

## Speed (lean hard)

Hormozi: the more he’s studied behavior/sales/marketing, the more he leans into **speed**.  
Humans are immediate-reward focused. Almost every business can offer a speed version.

**Marketplace entry heuristic:** look at what everyone else does → do it in **half the time**.

**Same work, different priority = margin:**  
Example — YouTube thumbnails: 1 week normal → +50% for 24h → +300% for 60 minutes.  
Same cost to produce; priority access is the product. Speed is a pricing lever.

---

## Effort / ease (two pieces)

Ease is not only “make it simple.” Two sides:

1. **Remove what they hate** — bad things they get to *not* do because they bought  
2. **Don’t block what they enjoy** — don’t force them to give up liked things if you can avoid it

**Gym counterexample (hard sale):**  
You must do things you hate *and* stop margaritas / taco Tuesday / cupcakes / McFlurries.  
Give up likes + do hates = brutal value equation.

---

## Principles he takes to the bank

- Fast sells better than slow  
- Easy sells better than hard  
- Risk-free sells better than risky  

Stick to few always-true principles; rest is application.  
Data often surprises intuition — don’t invent “because” stories beyond the context you measured.

## Sharp Flow application (notes for paywall / offers)

**Dream outcome (ours):** win more by pressing when proven money + conviction are real, fading loser money / thin spots — *without* becoming sharp yourself.

**Speed:** lock alerts to phone · board before tipoff · before market moves (vs finding out after / morning tip dumps).

**Effort:** we track wallets, size vs usual, for/against, limits/steam, unit ladder — they don’t build datasets / stack 3–4 subs / grind film.

**Likelihood / risk-free:** public lock book · can’t hide a loss · free trial · cancel easy · money-back · sized conviction record (e.g. 5u+ book) as proof.

**Do not** sell “picks.” Sell the dream + make speed/effort/risk obvious in the copy.

**Gym trap for us:** don’t make membership feel like more homework (another Discord to check). Phone ping + sized lock = remove hate-work, keep the part they like (betting when confident).
