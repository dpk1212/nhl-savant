# Hormozi SPCL — influence framework (canon)

**Source locked:** Alex Hormozi live teaching (transcript saved 2026-08-23 by Dale).  
**Letters:** **S·P·C·L** = **Status · Power · Credibility · Likeness**  
(say “special” if you want — not “problem/solution”).

Pairs with: `hormozi_qual_algorithm.md` (Qual · Algorithm · Trust economy).

**Goal of content (Hormozi definition):**  
**Influence** = *high likelihood of compliance with a future request*  
(subscribe / watch next / soft trial / “grade it yourself” — not max vanity views).

Any one letter gives some influence. **Stack all four** = max influence (“super SPCL”).

Do **not** conflate with older shorthand “PSCL = Problem/Solution/…” — that was wrong for this framework. Problem→proof→ask is still useful *craft order*; **SPCL is the influence stack**.

---

## S — Status

**Operational definition (observable):**  
Status = you **control reinforcers / scarce resources** people want *in a given environment*.

- Bartender in a busy bar = status (controls drinks). Outside the bar = near zero.
- Money you control = status even if unearned (trust-fund kid). Continuum, not binary.
- One event can stamp multiple letters (e.g. $100M launch → Status + Credibility; teaching that works → Power).

**Sharp Flow / X — how we show Status**
- We control a scarce resource strangers want: **live positions of proven sharp wallets** (months of tape, sized bets, lock board) — not vibes, not tipster secrets.
- Open heroes with **observable scarce proof**: L30 $, unopposed proven money, public pile vs our board, units on a locked play.
- Flex without scarcity = weak Status (“I have opinions”). Control of the feed of sharps = Status.

**Hero test:** Can a stranger *see* what scarce thing we control in ≤2 lines?

---

## P — Power (Hormozi’s #1 if forced to pick one)

**Operational definition:**  
**Say–do correspondence.** You say X → they do X → a reinforcing event happens → they are more likely to comply with the *next* request.

- Crypto picks that pop → Power + Status.
- Trust-fund money alone → Status without Power → less influence when you ask.

**Sharp Flow / X — how we build Power**
- Every graded win after “watch this wallet / this shape” is a reinforcement cycle.
- Confession + honest L next to W builds Power too (avoided bad outcome / trust that we don’t hide blemishes) — see `THE +100U IS NOT THE RECEIPT. THE 5.4U SPARKS LOSS IS.`
- Soft ask only works after enough say–do cycles. **Free proof in hero** = planting the next cycle; **ask in self-reply** = the request.

**Hero test:** If they followed the last 3 things we pointed at, did good things happen (or bad things get avoided)? Show that trail.

## C — Credibility

**Operational definition:**  
**Third-party / hard-to-fake observables** — something you can see with your eyes, not emotional adjectives.

- Guinness judges on site for book launch = paid third-party verification.
- $10M building behind you in an ad = hard to fake.
- Company sale can stamp Status (money) + Credibility (event happened).

**Sharp Flow / X — how we show Credibility**
- Screenshots of the product UI (L30 spark, battle bars, lock card) — stranger-verifiable.
- Named $ / WR / size vs usual / beat-close % from tracked wallets — not “elite vibes.”
- Mute-test: strip numbers — spine must still hold; numbers must be real reads from this run.

**Hero test:** Is there a third-party-shaped receipt (wallet tape / board split / lock card) a skeptic can’t shrug off as our opinion?

---

## L — Likeness

**Operational definition:**  
Psychological match (shared values / “vibe” / behavior set that matched positive people in their past) **or** literal similarity (look like the avatar). Continuum.

- Leila vs Alex same topics, different likeness → different gender mix in audience.
- Parents max SPCL early: scarce resources + endless say–do + some credibility + they literally look like you.

**Sharp Flow / X — how we show Likeness**
- **Be Dale** — dinner-table, costly honesty, anti-NPC. Zero ROI pretending to be Trent/Beast/tipster archetype.
- Lean into nuances you can defend (lock discipline, show the L, fade theater).
- Raw > over-polished when stacking influence (Hormozi: live/interactive direction). On X: human voice + unfinished stakes > corporate tipster chrome.

**Hero test:** Would Dale say this out loud to a stranger — or is it an NPC sportsbook costume?

## Content system laws (from same teaching)

### 1. Right content > more views
- Grandma slap video = views for humor watchers, **wrong prospects**.
- **Content is the targeting.** Algorithms route by topic/look/history.
- Educator (us) ≠ entertainer. Optimize for **IRL compliance** from bettors who want proof — not Mr. Beast view comps.

### 2. Interest media (not “social” vanity)
- Make content **for the avatar** (sports bettors who want wallet-level proof).
- Low views with right room > high views with wrong room.
- Measure: texts/replies from people you’d want as customers (“that was fire”) > raw impression ego.

### 3. Volume is linear (don’t outsmart the work)
- Hormozi org: ~100× content volume vs a 1–2 person shop → ~100× prospects when SPCL boxes are checked.
- Outliers are a known rate — jam inputs while staying on-avatar.

### 4. Time-with-prospect = reinforcement cycles
- 2 hours long-form ≫ hundreds of 15s shorts for say–do exposure.
- Shorts’ job often = **earn the click into a long** (risk of time).
- Live/interactive ≈ rawer truth, more cycles, more influence (Hormozi media bet).
- **X map:** hero = short-to-medium SPCL stamp; threads / SR / replies / outbound under velocity = more cycles; never auto-publish.

### 5. Ask sizing
- Influence needed scales with ask size (like / follow / soft trial / paid).
- Stack SPCL **before** bigger asks. Our law unchanged: **desire + proof in hero · soft ask + link in self-reply only.**

### 6. Domain specificity
- Fashion tips from a money guy = low SPCL in that domain.
- We have max SPCL on **sharp-wallet / board-money / sized-conviction** topics. Stay there.

**Kill:** vanity hooks that attract wrong avatar; Status flex without Power trail; Credibility adjectives without observables; Likeness cosplay.

## Free vs ask (influence economics)

1. **Give** SPCL proof until say–do cycles exist.
2. **Ask** only at the level your SPCL supports (soft trial ≪ hard sell).
3. Wrong content → wrong audience → ask fails even with clever CTA.
4. Right content + volume → compounding Power → asks get cheaper.
