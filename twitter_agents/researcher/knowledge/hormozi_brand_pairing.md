## What branding is (operational)

```
product  +  action they take  +  yum (outcome they like)
         =  pairing
         =  brand
```

Coca-Cola: drink it → yum → reach for Coke next time you want yum.

**Advertising** = people find out about the stuff.  
**Branding** = what that stuff gets paired with after they see it / use it.

Good branding = pair Sharp Flow with **good outcomes for sports bettors who want proof**.  
Bad branding = pair Sharp Flow with outcomes / identities that audience hates or that are random (tipster theater, convert-as-open, fade-Under-as-religion, V12-as-identity).

What we pair determines:

1. **Who** pays attention
2. **Direction** — toward us or away

Brand scoreboard (Hormozi): **influence** (does seeing us change behavior) · **direction** (toward) · **reach** (how many). Our north star stays **free trials / compliance**, not impressions.

---

## Bouquet (how a brand is assembled)

Each post / product / person / result is a flower. Repeated pairing with the *same* yum makes a bouquet. Distant flowers (taco + bike + socks) are not a brand.

**Pull weeds.** One ugly pairing (gratitude-as-open, promo-code hero, “best kept secret”) taints the bouquet. You don’t erase it — you overwhelm it with the pairing we actually want.

Product after purchase does most of the branding. Premium that doesn’t deliver loses the second buy. Live locks that miss still brand us if we show the L (whole truth). Live locks that hit brand us only if the *pairing* was “you weren’t sitting with the dead pile” — not “Dale called an Over.”

## Sharp Flow pairing (locked)

| Pair Sharp Flow with | Do not pair with |
|----------------------|------------------|
| Being on the side the **losing money isn’t** | Tipster “we have a lock tonight” as identity |
| Sizing up with proven wallets **without becoming sharp** | Become-sharp homework / data-feed lecture |
| Proof you can grade yourself | SUMMER / UPGRADE / convert paragraph as the open |
| Tonight’s board, walked in public | System labels (V12, Vault, Tier A, AGS) |
| **This total’s** dead pile vs our side | Over-as-brand or Under-as-brand (we lock both) |

**Yum for this product:** you get put on the sharp tickets. You don’t buy a feed. You don’t buy a capper. You don’t sit with the loud losing pile.
