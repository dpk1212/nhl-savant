## Why this is the 2026 move for our product

Picks / bankroll advice is **high-risk content** (money). AI and tipster GPT can copy a take. They cannot copy **us walking a live board**.

Accomplishments (`+60u`, Dom’s ledger) you mention once. **Demonstration** you do every lock: document the work while you deliver it.

If the room is still cold: “Let me do a whole bunch of work for free. I’ll document it and give it away.”

That *is* a Sharp Flow hero give. We are not “making content.” We are capturing the audit.

## Start free (Ep 990)

Always start free. Free users pay you three ways: they leave proof · they refer · they stay when it stops being free.

Hidden cost of a betting product is usually **not the subscription price** — it’s the homework, the data, the capper they have to follow. Our dream already kills that cost: size up without becoming sharp. **Show the work being done for them.** Do not ask what they’re paying for data (CSV: convert-as-open = 1 eng).

## Proof over promise (Ep 990)

Promises approximate likelihood. Proof *is* likelihood.

Crazy grand-slam stack + no testimonials loses to the same product + a wall of proof. Our UPGRADE / SUMMER / “we’ve got you covered” as **line 1** is promise. Same words after pudding can be a close when PURPOSE=ask.

## Four elements of compelling proof

| Element | Meaning | Sharp Flow |
|---------|---------|------------|
| **1. Recent** | Last week > five years | Tonight’s split + yesterday’s cash. Not August printer on a live lock. |
| **2. Visual** | Screenshot / tape > adjectives | Attach the map, the lock, the wallet tape. Copy names what the eye should see. |
| **3. Volume** | Overwhelm with receipts you already have | Many numbers, **one** idea. Toss salad is fake volume. |
| **4. Pain first** | Start where they are now. Opening on the result is too far away and less believable | They are staring at the **loud** side. Open on that pile. Classify it as dead. Then show we locked the other side. |

Pain-first and Status can live in the same two lines: the $ they already see, classified by the scarce thing we control (losing $ vs our $).

## Demonstrate ≠ caption

| Caption (weak) | Audit (strong) |
|----------------|----------------|
| Three screenshots + a label | Walk the board in the same order the images fire |
| “We locked 3u” as the idea | The work: loud pile → losing vs proven → lock |
| Recap of a printer | Live delivery, captured |

Attach order should match the walk (pain they see → decision → say–do tape), not “prettiest card first.”
